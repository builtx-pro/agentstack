# Community conduct

Help people understand and improve the work. Be specific, respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers and different levels of technical experience.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private customer data and abusive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation. Report sensitive concerns privately through the repository's security reporting channel when disclosure would put someone at risk.

Disagree with ideas and implementation choices without attacking people. Give credit where it is due, and be clear about what you have verified.
